`blur` softens an image with a Gaussian filter, for backgrounds, privacy and smoothing. `sharpen` applies an unsharp mask to luminance to restore crispness lost when shrinking.

## blur \{#blur}
`blur(sigma:precision:minAmplitude:)`; plain `blur()` is a mild blur with `sigma: 1`.

| Parameter | Type | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `sigma` | `Double` | `1` | Blur radius in pixels; finite and above zero. |
| `precision` | `BlurPrecision` | `.integer` | `.integer`, `.float`, `.approximate`: trades accuracy for speed. |
| `minAmplitude` | `Double` | `0.2` | Finite, above zero and at most `10`; smaller gives a wider, more accurate mask. |

Pixels are weighted by alpha, so transparent areas do not bleed colour into the blur: the emblem's edges soften without a dark fringe. Larger `sigma` costs more time and memory per pixel.

## sharpen \{#sharpen}
`sharpen(sigma:)` is the short form (`sigma` defaults to `1`). `sharpen(_ options: SharpenOptions)` exposes the unsharp-mask controls. Alpha is untouched.

| `SharpenOptions` | Default | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `sigma` | `1` | Radius of the Gaussian used to find detail, in pixels; above zero. |
| `flat` | `0` | Strength applied to flat areas. |
| `jagged` | `3` | Strength applied to steep edges. |
| `threshold` | `2` | Difference that separates flat areas from jagged edges. |
| `maxBrightening` | `10` | The most a pixel may brighten. |
| `maxDarkening` | `20` | The most a pixel may darken. |

`flat`, `jagged`, `threshold`, `maxBrightening` and `maxDarkening` are finite values from `0` to `1,000,000`; the defaults are libvips' own.

## Order and combinations \{#combinations}
- **Sharpen after resizing, not before.** Shrinking softens an image, and sharpening a full-size image just before a large reduction is partly thrown away by the resize. See [Order of operations](/oss/hokusai/interactions/resize-blur-sharpen).
- Blur before a large reduction is wasted work as well: reduce first, then blur at the final scale.
- `blur` and `sharpen` do not change the image size; `sharpen` leaves alpha untouched, and `blur` weights pixels by alpha.

## Errors \{#errors}
`blur(sigma: 0)` throws `HokusaiError.invalidOption` ("must be a finite value above zero"), as do a non-finite `sigma`, a `minAmplitude` outside `(0, 10]`, and out-of-range `SharpenOptions`. All are thrown when the pipeline is built.
