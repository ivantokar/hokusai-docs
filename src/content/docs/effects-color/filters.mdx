## Reference \{#reference}
| Operation | Parameters | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `median(size:)` | `3`; `1…101` | Each pixel becomes the median of its `size × size` neighbourhood. |
| `convolve(_ kernel:)` | `ConvolutionKernel` | Output = (sum of neighbourhood × `values`) ÷ `scale` + `offset`, limited to the sample range; edge pixels read the nearest edge pixel. |
| `linear(multiply:add:)` | `1`, `0`; `multiply` `−1000…1000`, `add` `−65535…65535` | Per-sample `x · multiply + add` on a 0…255 scale (16-bit scales it), clamped. |
| `recombine(_ matrix:)` | `ColorMatrix` | 3 × 3 mix of red, green, blue (read as sRGB). Presets `.sepia`, `.luminance`, `.swapRedBlue`, or nine finite coefficients. |
| `dilate(radius:)` | `1`; `1…50` | Each sample becomes the maximum of the `2 · radius + 1` square: grows bright areas. |
| `erode(radius:)` | `1`; `1…50` | The minimum: shrinks bright areas. |
| `edges(_ detector:)` | `.sobel`, `.scharr`, `.prewitt`, `.canny(sigma:)` | Strength of luminance change as **one** channel. Canny `sigma` in `0.1…20`, default `1.4`. |

### ConvolutionKernel \{#kernel}
`ConvolutionKernel(width:height:values:scale:offset:)` has sides `1…51` and exactly `width × height` finite `values` (row by row, top to bottom); `scale` is finite and not zero (default `1`), `offset` finite (default `0`). It throws `invalidOption` otherwise. A kernel whose coefficients sum to **1** keeps brightness (sharpening: `[0,-1,0,-1,5,-1,0,-1,0]`); one that sums to **0** (edges, emboss) leaves flat areas black, so give it an `offset` such as `128` to centre the result on grey, as the emboss example does.

### Morphology \{#morphology}
`dilate` and `erode` work on each channel separately, so they are most useful on a black-and-white mask (made with [`threshold`](/oss/hokusai/effects-color/tone-and-color)). `erode` then `dilate` removes specks; `dilate` then `erode` closes small gaps. Above, `dilate` removes the thin black lighthouse stripes of the mask and `erode` widens them.

## Errors \{#errors}
Out-of-range values throw `HokusaiError.invalidOption` when the pipeline is built: `median(size: 200)`, `dilate(radius: 60)`, `edges(.canny(sigma: 50))` (see [Tone & color](/oss/hokusai/effects-color/tone-and-color#errors) for the measured messages). A malformed kernel or matrix throws when you construct it.
