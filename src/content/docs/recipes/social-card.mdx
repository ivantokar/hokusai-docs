**Goal.** Turn a photo and a headline into the 1200 × 630 image that chat apps and social networks show for a link. **Inputs:** [`scene.png` and `phone.jpg`](https://github.com/ivantokar/hokusai-docs/tree/main/examples/fixtures). **Needs:** Hokusai 2.2.2 with text rendering (`capabilities().textRendering`).

## How it works \{#how}
1. **`autoOrient()`** makes a phone photo upright before anything measures it, so the same function works for any upload ([why](/oss/hokusai/interactions/orientation-and-resize)).
2. **`resize(width: 1200, height: 630, fit: .cover, position: .attention)`** fills the card exactly and crops to the interesting part: the lighthouse stays in frame even though it is off-centre ([position](/oss/hokusai/geometry/resize#position)).
3. **`drawText(…)`** places the title with `.gravity(.southWest, offsetX:, offsetY:)` so it stays 56 px from the left and 48 px from the bottom whatever the card size. `layout: .width(900)` wraps long titles; the shadow keeps white text legible over any sky ([text](/oss/hokusai/text-composition/text)).
4. **`webp(quality: 82)`** for the web, and **JPEG** as a fallback for crawlers.

## Variations \{#variations}
- **Longer titles:** `.width(900)` wraps at 900 px; lower `fontSize` or use `.fit(width: 900, height: 200)` to guarantee the text fits a box.
- **Different aspect ratios** (1:1, 1.91:1): change only the `resize` box.
- **A photo with a dark sea and a bright sky:** keep the shadow, or `composite` a darkening `.multiply` layer under the text ([blend modes](/oss/hokusai/text-composition/composite#blend)).
- **Custom fonts:** `TextStyle(fontFile:)` when the host does not have your font installed.
