**Goal.** From any photo, make a 256 × 256 circular avatar. **Inputs:** [`scene.png`, `emblem.png`](https://github.com/ivantokar/hokusai-docs/tree/main/examples/fixtures) (its alpha channel is the circle). **Needs:** Hokusai 2.2.2.

## How it works \{#how}
1. **`resize(width: 256, height: 256, fit: .cover, position: .attention)`** makes the square and keeps the interesting part in it.
2. **A circular mask** is the alpha channel of an existing round image, resized to 256 px: `extractChannel(.alpha)` turns it into a grey image, white inside the disc and black outside.
3. **`withAlpha(from: mask)`** uses that luminance as the avatar's alpha: white keeps pixels, black hides them. The mask must come out the **same size** as the avatar, which is why both are 256 px ([channels](/oss/hokusai/channels-transparency/alpha-and-channels#with-alpha)).
4. **PNG or WebP keep the transparency**; for places that cannot show alpha, `flatten(background:)` onto the page colour and encode JPEG ([why flatten last](/oss/hokusai/interactions/flatten-before-jpeg)).

## Variations \{#variations}
- **A different shape:** any image with an alpha channel or a grey image of the right size works as the mask (a rounded square, a hexagon).
- **A soft edge:** `blur(sigma: 2)` on the mask before `withAlpha` feathers the edge.
- **Retina:** build at 512 and let the page scale it down.
