**Goal.** Stamp a semi-transparent logo in a corner of every published photo, and offer a faint all-over tile for proofs. **Inputs:** [`scene.png`, `phone.jpg`, `emblem.png`](https://github.com/ivantokar/hokusai-docs/tree/main/examples/fixtures). **Needs:** Hokusai 2.2.2.

## How it works \{#how}
- **The mark is prepared once** (`mark`, resized to 120 px) and reused: a layer is just a pipeline, so it is evaluated per image without being re-encoded ([composition](/oss/hokusai/text-composition/composite)).
- **`autoOrient()` and `resize` come before `composite`**, so the mark has the same size on every output and the placement is measured on the final image ([order](/oss/hokusai/interactions/composite-order)).
- **`.gravity(.southEast, offsetX: 20, offsetY: 20)`** keeps the mark 20 px from the corner regardless of the photo's size; `opacity: 0.7` lets the photo show through.
- JPEG output has no alpha, but the photos are opaque, so no `flatten` is needed here. If the base can be transparent, flatten last ([why](/oss/hokusai/interactions/flatten-before-jpeg)).

## Variations \{#variations}
- **A proof tile:** `.tile(gravity: .center)` with `opacity: 0.18` repeats a small mark over the whole image (the third result).
- **Scale the mark to the photo:** `mark.resize(width: photoWidth / 8)`.
- **Text as the watermark:** replace the layer with `Hokusai.text(…)` ([text](/oss/hokusai/text-composition/text)).
- **A darkening corner for legibility:** a second layer with `.multiply` under the mark.
