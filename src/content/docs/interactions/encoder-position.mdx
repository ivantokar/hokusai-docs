`jpeg()`, `png()`, `webp()`, `avif()`, `heif()`, `tiff()`, `gif()`, `pdf()` and `metadataPolicy(_:)` are **terminal configuration**, not image operations. They do not change pixels, so their place in the chain does not matter, and the last one wins.

<Example id="order.encoder" />

The program printed:

- `encoder first == encoder last: true`: `jpeg(quality: 70).resize(width: 300)` and `resize(width: 300).jpeg(quality: 70)` produced byte-identical output.
- `png() then webp() → webp`: selecting a second encoder replaces the first.

By convention put the encoder last, where it reads as the end of the recipe. Put it early only when a helper returns a pipeline with its encoder chosen. A layer's encoder and metadata settings are ignored when it is composed.
