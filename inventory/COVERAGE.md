## Native capability restrictions

Not Hokusai gaps: they depend on the installed native libraries.

| Capability | Note | Family |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HEIF / AVIF, JPEG XL, JPEG 2000, SVG and PDF input, other optional codecs | Depend on how libvips was built, not on Hokusai. `Hokusai.capabilities()` and `hokusai info` report them. | encoding-output |
| Pango text rendering and font files | Needs a libvips with Pango; `TextStyle.fontFile` also needs fontconfig and a wrong file is only a libvips warning. | text-composition |
| Magic Kernel resize kernels | libvips 8.17 or newer; reported instead of assumed. | geometry |
| AVIF orientation as container rotation | Written by libvips 8.18, not by the 8.16 baseline; call autoOrient() first on older builds. | encoding-output |
| HokusaiPDF | Needs Pango 1.44 or newer; the Hokusai product does not. | pdf |
